# CHILD SAFETY SOP

## Before booking
Confirm parent/guardian authority, Child age, allergies/sensitivities, responsible adult presence and voluntary participation.

## Before arrival
Review participant information, materials/SDS, room access, hazards, floor/furniture protection and emergency contact.

## Setup
Establish a controlled work zone; secure unsuitable materials; ensure ventilation; explain age-appropriate rules.

## Participation
Parent/Guardian remains responsible. Never force participation. Keep activities age-appropriate. Stop unsafe activity. Control access to wet/curing surfaces and tools.

## Incident
Stop activity; notify parent/guardian; provide only appropriate first-response assistance within training; seek emergency assistance when reasonably necessary; record and follow up.

## Photography
Do not photograph or publish another child without appropriate consent.

Core principle: prevention, adult supervision, suitable materials, controlled work areas and a child-centred approach.
