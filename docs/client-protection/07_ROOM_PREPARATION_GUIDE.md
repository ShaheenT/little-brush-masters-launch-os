# ROOM PREPARATION GUIDE

☐ Remove fragile/valuable objects.
☐ Clear furniture from the selected wall where possible.
☐ Remove loose wall décor.
☐ Keep pets away from the work area.
☐ Tell us about recent painting, repairs or dampness.
☐ Tell us about allergies/sensitivities.
☐ Ensure ventilation.
☐ Keep unrelated children away from the controlled work area.
☐ Have comfortable clothing ready.
☐ Have the Child ready at the agreed start time.

Tell us if the wall has damp, mould, peeling paint, cracks, loose plaster, wallpaper, recent repairs or unusual coatings. Additional preparation may require a separate quotation.

The Parent/Guardian remains responsible for the Child during the experience. Materials should be allowed to dry/cure according to manufacturer instructions.
