## 1. Parties and authority
The Client confirms that they are the parent/legal guardian or otherwise authorised adult responsible for the Child and have authority to enter into this agreement.

## 2. Service
Little Brush Masters provides a private family-participation creative experience involving the design and creation of artwork on an agreed wall or walls. The service is sold as a creative project and experience, not as an hourly painting service. Artistic variation from sketches or reference images is expected.

## 3. Child participation
The Child participates voluntarily and in an age-appropriate manner. Little Brush Masters is not a childcare, babysitting, education or medical service. A parent/guardian or responsible adult must remain present and responsible for the Child throughout the experience. Little Brush Masters may pause, modify or stop an activity where reasonably necessary for safety or wellbeing.

## 4. Materials and room
Materials will be selected for the intended application and used according to manufacturer instructions. The Client must disclose known allergies/sensitivities. The Client must provide reasonable access and remove fragile, valuable or obstructive items. Wall preparation outside the agreed scope may require an additional quotation.

## 5. Payment
A 50% deposit secures the project date unless otherwise agreed in writing. The balance is due before the experience begins. A booking is not confirmed until the deposit has cleared and written confirmation has been issued.

## 6. Cancellation and rescheduling
The current Little Brush Masters Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy applies. It will be applied reasonably and subject to applicable South African consumer law. Nothing in this agreement is intended to exclude or limit a right that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

## 7. Client responsibilities
The Client agrees to provide accurate information, disclose allergies/sensitivities, keep the Child under appropriate adult supervision, maintain a reasonably safe work area, keep pets/unrelated children away from controlled work areas when requested, follow reasonable safety instructions and obtain any necessary property-owner permissions.

## 8. Photography and media
Photography/video is governed by a separate consent process. Marketing consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing the service.

## 9. Privacy
Personal information will be processed according to the Little Brush Masters Privacy Notice and applicable South African privacy law.

## 10. Intellectual property
Unless otherwise agreed in writing, Little Brush Masters retains rights in original creative concepts, sketches, templates and brand materials. The completed commissioned physical artwork forms part of the Client's property once fully paid.

## 11. Safety
The Client acknowledges that creative activities involving paint and tools have practical risks. Little Brush Masters will take reasonable operational precautions. Nothing in this clause excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded.

## 12. Changes
Additional walls, significant preparation, material changes, additional design work or time outside scope require written approval and may affect price.
