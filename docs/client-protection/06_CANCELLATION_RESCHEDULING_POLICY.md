# CANCELLATION & RESCHEDULING POLICY

## Booking
A 50% deposit secures the agreed project date. The date is not held until the deposit clears and written confirmation is issued.

## Client cancellation
Because each project involves calendar allocation, preparation and customised creative work, cancellation may result in reasonable charges based on timing and costs already incurred. Little Brush Masters will consider the circumstances and applicable consumer-law requirements before applying a charge.

## Rescheduling
Where reasonably possible, Little Brush Masters will offer a replacement date. Requests should be made as early as possible and a particular replacement date is not guaranteed.

## Illness / child wellbeing
If the Child or an essential participant is unwell, contact Little Brush Masters promptly. Rescheduling may be recommended where participation could reasonably create a health or safety concern.

## Little Brush Masters cancellation
If Little Brush Masters cannot provide the service and an alternative date is not reasonably acceptable, amounts paid will be handled according to the agreement and applicable law.

## Scope changes
Additional walls, substantial preparation or additional design work may require a quotation.

Nothing in this policy is intended to waive or restrict a right or remedy that cannot lawfully be waived or restricted under South African law.
