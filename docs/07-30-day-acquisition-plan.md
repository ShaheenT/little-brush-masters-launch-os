# 30-Day Acquisition Plan

## Target

10 booked families.
R98,500 target revenue at R9,850 average project value.

## Week 1 — Launch

Daily:
- 10 direct partner outreaches
- 5 qualified parent conversations
- 3 Instagram Stories
- 1 strong Reel every 2–3 days
- Follow up every open lead

Partner targets:
- Interior designers
- Architects
- Luxury estate agents
- Property developers
- Premium children's furniture/interior brands
- Parenting communities
- Private schools

## Week 2 — Proof

Publish:
- Behind-the-scenes
- Child/parent participation
- Concept sketches
- Finished room
- Founder story
- FAQ
- First testimonial

## Week 3 — Referral

Ask every completed family for:
- testimonial
- approved photos
- one referral introduction

## Week 4 — Scarcity

Promote remaining monthly availability.
Never say "cheap", "discount", "painting rate", or "per square metre".

## Funnel targets

30 qualified enquiries
20 consultations
15 proposals
10 bookings

Primary KPI:
Average Revenue Per Family (ARPF).

Secondary KPIs:
Lead-to-consultation conversion.
Consultation-to-booking conversion.
Referral rate.
Partner-generated leads.
