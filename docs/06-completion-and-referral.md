# Completion + Referral Flow

Immediately after the reveal:

1. Photograph child + wall.
2. Photograph parent + child + wall.
3. Capture 10–20 detail images.
4. Ask for a short testimonial while emotion is high.
5. Send a thank-you message within 24 hours.
6. Request permission to use approved photographs.
7. Introduce referral credit.

Suggested message:

"We hope today becomes one of those little family memories that stays with you for years.

If you know another family who would love to create their own Childhood Project, we'd be delighted to welcome them.

When a family you refer books, we'll add R750 Little Brush Creative Credit to your account as our thank-you."

Do not discount the core project price.
