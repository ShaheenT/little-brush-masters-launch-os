# Operational Safety Checklist

Before each project:
- Confirm adult supervision requirements.
- Confirm participating children and ages.
- Confirm paint/product safety information for all materials used.
- Confirm ventilation.
- Protect floors, furniture and fixtures.
- Keep materials secured when not in use.
- Keep children away from tools/materials not intended for participation.
- Confirm emergency contact details.
- Obtain required photography/media consent.
- Confirm client accepts wall preparation requirements.

This checklist is operational guidance, not legal advice. Have the final client agreement and child-safety procedures reviewed for the applicable South African requirements before scaling.
