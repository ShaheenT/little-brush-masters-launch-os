# Qualification Script

Goal: determine fit before quoting.

Ask:
- Child's age?
- What are they passionate about?
- What is changing in the room?
- What would make them excited to see the finished room?
- Will one or both parents participate?
- When would they like the experience?
- One wall or larger transformation?

Budget positioning:

"Our private experiences begin at R6,850, with bespoke multi-wall experiences from R9,850. Would that investment be within the range you're considering?"

If yes, continue to consultation.
If no, thank them and do not discount the core offer.
