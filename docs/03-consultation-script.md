# 15–20 Minute Consultation

1. "Tell me about your child."
2. "What do they love right now?"
3. "What do you want them to feel when they walk into the room?"
4. "Is there a family story or interest we could incorporate?"
5. "How much of the experience would you like to participate in?"
6. "What does the room currently look like?"

Recommendation:

"Based on what you've described, I'd recommend the [PACKAGE]. We would design the artwork as one connected story rather than treating it as separate painted walls."

State price once. Then pause.

Booking:
- 50% deposit to secure the date.
- Balance due before the experience begins.
- Date is not reserved until deposit clears.
